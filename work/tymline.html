The Challenge

Representing information online is messy: blogs are heavy, feeds are ephemeral. People needed a way to organise media and links chronologically around topics - and share them.

What we built

From sketch to shipped in 90 days

A complete web application designed from first sketches through multiple design-language iterations - components, pages, and all.

01

Timeline Creation

Multiple customisable timelines per user, organised by theme.

02

Chronological Media

Posts, media, and links arranged in clean time order.

03

Shareable by Design

Changelogs, feature pipelines, segmented blogs - one format, many uses.

04

Component Design

Post components and page headers built as a reusable system.
