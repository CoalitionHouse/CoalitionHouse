The Challenge

Swasth runs free health checkups, workshops, and wellness activities for low-income families - but field operations ran on paper. Data capture was slow, error-prone, and hard to coordinate across agents and communities.

What we built

Field-first, by design

A progressive web app for field data collection - shaped by joining the NGO's health drives in person and translating real-world process into product.

01

On-ground Research

Joined field operations and health drives to map how agents actually work.

02

03

04

Process Translation

Real-world NGO workflows turned into simple, usable screens.
